A dense Monday-edition broadsheet front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026 in a single image: 33 stories, 13 illustrated, drawn from 40+ verified reports (AP, Reuters, CNN, BBC, AFP, Al Jazeera, WSJ, Le Monde and agency wires), each opened and date-checked before inclusion.

Hero: COP31 ends with no deal — the Antalya text collapsed in overtime after the EU, Panama, the Marshall Islands, Norway and Vanuatu rejected a draft with no fossil-fuel mention. Also on the front page: the Sparta II towed out of Finnish waters with all 16 crew safe, the Russian corvette Merkury sunk at Benghazi, and twenty numbered briefs across Europe, the Middle East, the Americas, Asia, Africa and Oceania.

- `entry.png` — the entry image (1024×1024, as the gate requires)
- `news-image-20261123.png` — the hi-res master render (2400×4600)
- `generate.py` — the generator that draws the page (original procedural PIL artwork)
- `STORIES.md` — the 33 stories as they appear on the page
- `SOURCES.md` — every story's source outlet, article date and verification note
